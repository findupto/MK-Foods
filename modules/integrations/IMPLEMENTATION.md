# Integrations

Use provider adapters with normalized internal models and durable queues.

- Delivery: Uber Eats, DoorDash and additional aggregator adapters where APIs/contracts are available.
- Communications: SMS and email providers with consent-aware campaign queues.
- Accounting: accounting export/API adapters.
- Payments: EMV/NFC/wallet/gateway adapters.
- Maps: geocoding, routing and GPS adapters.

Third-party credentials are encrypted and never hard-coded. Provider outages must not corrupt local orders; unsupported providers remain disabled until configured.
