# Suppliers & Procurement

- Supplier CRUD, contacts, lead times, payment terms and product catalog.
- Purchase order lifecycle: draft → approved → sent → partially received → received → closed/cancelled.
- Goods receiving with barcode scan, quantity variance, damaged/short/extra handling.
- Supplier invoices, three-way matching where configured, due dates and partial payments.
- Accounts payable balance and payment history.
- Supplier performance: fill rate, lead time, shortages, quality, price history and delivery reliability.
