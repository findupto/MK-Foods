# Menu Management

## Categories & Items
- Organize products into categories and subcategories.
- Item name, description, image, SKU/barcode, recipe, modifiers, allergens, pricing, tax class, and channel availability.

## Pricing & Taxes
- Configure base and location-specific prices.
- Discounts and promotional pricing are permission-controlled and effective-dated.
- Tax rules support the business default of 0% and future regional/location-specific rates.
- Price/configuration versions are published to edge nodes and can be rolled back.

## Availability
- Mark items available, unavailable, scheduled, or sold out.
- Availability may be driven by inventory thresholds.
- Channel-specific availability supports POS, kiosk, delivery, and other ordering channels.
- Edge nodes retain the last valid menu version for offline operation.
