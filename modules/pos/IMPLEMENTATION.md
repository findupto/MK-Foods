# POS Workflow

- Hold/resume named or numbered orders.
- Modifiers and free-text item notes.
- Discount rules: item/order, fixed/percentage, approval thresholds, reason and audit trail.
- Tax calculation by tax class; business default remains 0%.
- Split bills by item, quantity, seat, guest, or amount.
- Partial payments with multiple tenders and remaining balance.
- Refunds and reversals linked to original receipt/payment reference.
- Voids require permission and reason; completed financial records remain immutable.
- Receipt history with search, reprint, preview, and duplicate-print audit.
- Cash drawer open/close events, opening float, paid-in/out, cash sales, expected cash, counted cash, variance and manager approval.
- End-of-day reconciliation closes the business day only after payment, drawer, refund, and variance checks.
- All financial operations generate durable local events for synchronization.
