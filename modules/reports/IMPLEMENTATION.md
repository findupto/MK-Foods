# Reporting & Analytics

Reports include sales, products, profit/margin, tax, inventory valuation, cashier performance, rider performance and kitchen performance.

Time dimensions: hourly, daily, weekly, monthly and custom date ranges.

Filters: location, cashier, rider, category, item, order type, payment type and status.

Exports: CSV, XLSX and PDF through dedicated export services. Exports are permission-controlled and audited.

Analytics provide trend charts, top/bottom products, average order value, gross margin, prep-time distribution, delivery SLA, stock movement and cash variance.
