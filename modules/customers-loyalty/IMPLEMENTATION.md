# Customers & Loyalty

- Customer CRUD, fast search and duplicate detection by phone/email.
- Purchase history linked to immutable receipts/orders.
- Configurable points earning, redemption, expiry and reversal rules.
- VIP tiers based on configurable spend/visits/points criteria.
- Store credit ledger with balance, issue, redemption and reversal events.
- SMS/email campaigns through provider adapters.
- Explicit marketing consent, opt-out, communication preferences and consent audit history.
- Offline customer lookup and queued updates with conflict-safe synchronization.
