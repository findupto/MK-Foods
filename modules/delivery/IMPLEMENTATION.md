# Delivery

- Real rider accounts with secure authentication and role permissions.
- Rider mobile app with offline delivery queue and synchronized status events.
- GPS consent, periodic location updates, last-known position and live map when online.
- Delivery zones, capacity and rider availability rules.
- Route optimization provider adapter with manual fallback.
- COD collection, settlement, variance and approval workflow.
- Failed delivery reasons, customer unreachable, cancelled, returned-to-store and reattempt workflows.
- Proof-of-delivery support where configured.
- Delivery SLA and rider performance analytics.
